# Brag Plan: Nexus

## What is this app?
Nexus is one chat window for every AI — type once, and it answers through the strongest available lane, switching providers automatically when one fails.

## The angle
The video doesn't sell "AI chat" in the abstract. It shows the one moment that makes Nexus different: a provider dies mid-conversation and the user never has to care — the answer just arrives anyway. One chat, one switch, no noise.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The landing page's own hero line, full-screen, no chrome: "Chat with every AI, from one Nexus." It is the product's promise and the video's thesis in one breath.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A real chat flow: the example prompt types itself, sends, and an answer streams back.
- The failover beat: a lane fails on screen, Nexus switches automatically, the answer continues — the product's signature moment, shown, not claimed.
- Three verbatim feature lines arriving one by one: "One shared memory", "Automatic failover", "Your keys, your vault".

## Outro / punchline
The Nexus wordmark, the hero line once more, and the public address — plus the landing page's own shrug: "Get the app — or don't."

## User flow worth showing
Type a message → answer starts arriving → the lane fails → Nexus switches automatically → the answer completes. (The landing page's own demo flow: prompt, failed lane, answered lane.)

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — one chat, one switch, no noise
- Interpretation: slow reveals, long holds, generous letter-spacing, soft crossfades; restraint is the confidence. Nothing shouts; the failover beat does the talking.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #050505 (landing page body; panels #0d0d10)
- Accent: #38bdf8 (sky blue, landing hero glow + links)
- Text: #fafafa (primary), #a7a7b0 (muted)
- Display font: Space Grotesk
- Body font: Space Grotesk (JetBrains Mono for the failover trace lines, matching the dashboard's mono trace)
- Strongest visual element: the dark chat window with the sky-blue glow, and the failover trace — a failed lane struck through, the next lane answering

## Share copy (draft)
Nexus: chat with every AI, from one Nexus. When a lane fails, it switches automatically — you never notice. Get the app, or don't.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady and clean, ~110 BPM) — the polished pick
- Music treatment: starts at 0, steady 0.35 bed, fades out over the final 1.5s under the outro
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read (assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md, tempo 109.96 BPM). Strong-cue locks: third feature line lands at 13.11s; outro address line lands at 17.47s. Beat-grid for the three feature reveals: 10.93, 12.02, 13.11s — labels hold as a set afterward, so beat spacing never outruns reading. Non-text glow accents at 8.74s and 9.29s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass breathes the hero glow and the chat card's presence only. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments: typed prompt (soft key ticks), send tap (click), failover switch (quiet switch cue), answer arrival (soft drop), outro wordmark (one low bell)
- Restraint rule: no SFX on every word; at most one cue per beat, and silence under the hook line's hold

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.0s (0.0–3.0)
On screen: near-black background with a soft sky-blue glow. "Chat with every AI," fades up, then "from one Nexus." beneath it. No chrome, no logo yet — the line is the whole frame.
Sequential/interaction: none — two lines, second line follows the first by 0.5s, both hold settled to 3.0s (8 words ≈ 2.4s floor, met).
Audio intent: music bed only, low; the line lands in near-silence.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the line carry).
Music: vol-12 bed, 0.35.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The flow: type, fail, switch, answered — 7.0s (3.0–10.0)
On screen: a recreated Nexus chat window (dark panel, composer pill reading "Type a message..."). The example prompt types itself: "Explain failover like I'm five". A cursor taps send. An answer begins to stream. Then the trace line appears: "Lane failed — switching…" (generic lane labels; no real provider names, no real user data — the landing demo's provider names are replaced with neutral stand-ins). The trace updates: "Switched automatically — answered." The answer's key line settles: "If one lane goes quiet, Nexus asks the next."
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing (3.4–4.8), send tap (4.8), failover flash (5.5 fail, 6.2 switched), answer key line settles at 6.6s and holds to 10.0s (3.4s for 10 words — the reading floor governs, so the 8.74s strong cue is used for a non-text trace glow accent instead, plus a card glow accent at 9.29s).
Audio intent: key ticks under typing, a soft click on send, one quiet switch cue at the failover, a soft drop as the answer lands.
Audio-coupled idea: typed text with key ticks; simulated tap on send; failover switch cue.
Music: vol-12 bed continues.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Three truths — 6.0s (10.0–16.0)
On screen: three feature lines, verbatim from the landing page, arriving one by one and then held as a set: "One shared memory" / "Automatic failover" / "Your keys, your vault". Small sky-blue rule above each line. No other copy.
Sequential/interaction: yes — lines arrive at 10.93, 12.02, and 13.11s (beat-grid, third line beat-locked to the 13.11s strong cue); the full set holds settled from 13.11s to 16.0s, so no line is ever read against the clock.
Audio intent: one soft drop per line, quiet and even.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card sequence (drop per line).
Music: vol-12 bed continues.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4.0s (16.0–20.0)
On screen: "Nexus" wordmark large, landing at 16.3s. Beneath it, the hero line once more, small: "Chat with every AI, from one Nexus." Then the public address "nexusos.dpdns.org" and the landing page's shrug: "Get the app — or don't." — the address line lands at 17.47s (beat-locked to the strong cue).
Sequential/interaction: none — wordmark lands at 16.3s, tagline settles at 17.0s and holds to 20.0s (3.0s for 8 words — floor met), address line holds 17.47s to 20.0s (2.53s for a short label — floor met).
Audio intent: music fades out over 18.5–20.0s; one low bell as the wordmark lands.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo bell.
Music: vol-12 bed, fade-out.
Transition mood: fade to black at 20.0s.

**Music mood for this video:** steady, clean, warm corporate bed
**Audio summary:** A steady low bed throughout with five quiet motion-matched cues (key ticks, send click, switch, answer drop, logo bell) and a fade-out under the outro — the sound of a product that doesn't need to shout.

No secrets, keys, tokens, internal hostnames, real user names, or email addresses appear anywhere in this plan or the video. The landing demo's provider names are replaced with neutral "lane" labels; the chat example uses the landing page's own public example prompt; no real user content is shown.
