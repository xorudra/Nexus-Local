Why Nexus

Built for chats that
outlive any one model.

Other apps lock your history to a single provider. Nexus keeps one shared memory — so when a lane goes down or hits a limit, the conversation just continues somewhere else.

Automatic failover

When a provider errors or rate-limits, Nexus hands the chat to the next healthy lane with a [Handover] note. You never retype, never re-explain.

Shared memory

Up to 100K chars of context per chat — newest turns verbatim, older ones compressed locally. Identical on every provider, so switching lanes loses nothing.

Bring your own keys

Connect your own provider accounts into your encrypted vault. Your keys stay yours — chat prefers them, and revoking is one tap.
