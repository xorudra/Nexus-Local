Everything in one place

One dashboard houses the whole conversation — whichever AI is best for the next message, it continues right where the last one stopped.

One shared memory

Start a conversation with one AI and finish it with another. Nexus carries the full conversation across providers — when a chat moves, the next AI is handed the story so far, not a blank page.

Claude answers→limit reached→Gemini continues, same memory

Automatic failover

A provider hits its limit or goes quiet mid-chat? Nexus moves down the line and the reply still arrives — no dead ends, no copy-pasting your question into five tabs.

A trace on every reply

Each answer shows its working: which providers were tried, which one answered, how long each took, and the honest reason the others didn't. No silent swaps, ever.

Your keys, your vault

Connect the AIs you already have — paste your own provider keys, or sign in with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Everything is stored encrypted; chat prefers your key, and nothing is ever shown to anyone else.

Private by design

Nexus runs on its owner's own server, for a small circle of invited people. No public sign-ups, no ads, no tracking — your chats stay yours.

Perfect set of tools

The composer wears many hats. Pick a mode and Nexus routes the job to a provider that can actually do it — and tells you plainly when one can't.

An API of its own

Your other projects can call Nexus through its OpenAI-compatible /v1 endpoint — one service key, and the same shared memory, failover and lanes answer behind it. Point any OpenAI client at Nexus and it just works.

Get your service key 
curl https://nexus.130-210-56-74.sslip.io/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <your service key>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model": "nexus-auto",
       "messages": [{"role": "user",
                     "content": "Hello"}]}'
