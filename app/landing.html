Everything in one place

One dashboard houses the whole conversation — whichever AI is best for the next message, it continues right where the last one stopped.

One shared memory

Start a conversation with one AI and finish it with another. Nexus carries the full conversation across providers — when a chat moves, the next AI is handed the story so far, not a blank page.

Automatic failover

A provider hits its limit or goes quiet mid-chat? Nexus moves down the line and the reply still arrives — no dead ends, no copy-pasting your question into five tabs.

A trace on every reply

Each answer shows its working: which providers were tried, which one answered, how long each took, and the honest reason the others didn't. No silent swaps, ever.

Frequently asked questions

The short, honest answers — the same ones the app itself gives.

Is Nexus free to use?

Yes. Nexus runs on free tiers and its owner's own server — there is no subscription and nothing to buy. When a provider's free limit is reached, failover simply moves your chat to another lane.

Who can join?

Nexus is invite-only: the owner invites a small circle of people, and there is no public sign-up — by design. If you were invited, use Sign in above.

Which AIs can I use?

The ones Nexus routes to — ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, plus a bench of providers like Groq, NVIDIA, Mistral, Cohere, OpenRouter and Pollinations. Connect your own accounts and keys on the Connections page, and chat prefers them.

Where do my chats and keys live?

On the owner's server, encrypted. Saved keys are never displayed back after saving, and your chats belong to you — other members of the circle cannot see them.
