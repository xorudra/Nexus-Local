MY KEYS

Your personal provider keys. When set, your chats use your keys instead of the shared ones — your usage burns your own quotas, not the admin's. Keys are encrypted with your login password. Leave a field blank to keep its current value.

PROVIDER KEYS

Choose your lane — expand FreeLLMAPI or Relay and add your keys there. In the chat, each lane's providers appear separately, and your key works in the lane you saved it under.

FreeLLMAPI Providers

The normal direct lane — keys for Groq, Google, OpenRouter, NVIDIA and the freemium providers.

Relay Providers

The relay lane — the same big providers reached through the relay (different accounts/quotas than the FreeLLMAPI lane).
